Nothing Beats Seasonal Ingredients

As many seasonal ingredients as we can find, each at the very peak of its flavor.

We fry each ingredient to draw out its own true flavor.
We let its natural colors shine and plate every dish with care.
Taste, color, form and aroma — we take care of every one of them.

And wine is our secret seasoning, a gentle accent for seasonal ingredients.
Its aroma rises from the glass and meets the aroma of the food, revealing something new with every bite.

Please enjoy the simple pleasure of seasonal ingredients,
and the endless possibilities they open up alongside wine.

Rice Grown in Tamba

Our group’s agricultural company, Tamba Nosho Co., Ltd., grows rice in about 10 hectares of fields in Tamba, Hyogo. All the rice we serve in our restaurants is this Tamba-grown rice.

We enrich our rice paddies with compost from Tajima beef raised by Tamba Nosho, and use fewer pesticides. We also grow Tamba’s famous chestnuts—our autumn Tamba Chestnut Tempura is a seasonal favorite every year.

For our lunch pickles buffet, we use vegetables from local Tamba farmers.
