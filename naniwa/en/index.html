Signature Dishes

Takoyaki (Octopus Balls)

Our takoyaki features a fluffy, creamy texture and a savory dashi broth that tastes delicious even on its own. The batter is made with a blend of kombu and bonito-based dashi, offering an authentic flavor that's light yet full of umami.

Karaage (Japanese Fried Chicken)

We use three cuts: drumettes, wings, and breast. The wings and drumettes are packed with meat at the tip, while the breast is cut thick, allowing our secret dashi to soak in for a juicy finish.

Tako Dashi Oden (Octopus Broth Oden)

Our oden is slowly simmered in a rich octopus broth, warming both body and soul. Enjoy it as a starter before your takoyaki, or dip your takoyaki in the leftover broth for an extra treat.
